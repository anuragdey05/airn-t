airn't — how many cigarettes is the air you're breathing?

airn't turns the live air quality index (AQI / PM2.5) where you are into the number of cigarettes you are passively smoking today. The conversion follows Berkeley Earth: 22 µg/m³ of PM2.5 ≈ 1 cigarette per day.

Features

  • Live AQI → cigarettes per day for your GPS location or any city worldwide
  • City-vs-city comparison and a global leaderboard
  • 7-day history, 3-day forecast, and yearly pack equivalents
  • A satirical "should you light up?" verdict based on Pope et al. (2009) and Becker & Murphy (1988)
